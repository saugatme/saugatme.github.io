03 / ABOUT ME

A biomedical starting point.
A growing interest in computing.

I’m interested in how we turn a messy observation into something we can understand—and build on.

I started in biomedical engineering, working with the body, sensors and clinical equipment. My bachelor’s thesis was my first major opportunity to bring programming into that work: measuring movement from video using OpenCV and biomechanics.

At KTH, I’ve been exploring more of the computing around health data. I’ve especially enjoyed the practical challenges: preparing a useful dataset, making a pipeline run on a different operating system, or figuring out what needs to happen before a job can run on a supercomputer.

I’m still developing my direction as an ML engineer or researcher. Medical imaging is a particular interest, alongside the data preparation and engineering that make experiments possible. I’m looking for an internship or thesis opportunity where I can keep learning through substantive work.

Outside that, I enjoy airplanes, geography and Fantasy Premier League. Those interests also find their way into the projects I choose.
