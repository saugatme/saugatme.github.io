PERSONAL EXPLORATION / FOOTBALL & DATA

Looking at FPL beyond the points

I love Fantasy Premier League. This personal notebook explores the players behind the game, bringing together my interest in football, geography and working with data.

Explore source ↗

What the notebook explores

The notebook reads an FPL data snapshot and joins player records with team information. It looks at national representation among players who have played at least one minute, identifies the youngest and oldest players, and plots age and birth-date distributions.

The work uses Pandas for joining and organizing the data, with Matplotlib and Seaborn for charts. A second, shorter notebook inspects fixtures and players with the highest total points.

Exploring the FPL API

A related API Playground repository contains a Python client and data helpers for accessing player details, fixtures, gameweek information and league standings. It explores the data-access side of the same interest, including request pacing and season-dependent player identifiers.

Earlier exploration

The FPL 2024–25 repository ↗ preserves an earlier season of data and notebook work. It sits alongside the newer analysis and API experiments as part of the same personal interest.

Project scope

This is an exploratory personal project, not a finished prediction service. The reviewed repository contains substantive notebook analysis, while the planned model-training, prediction and dashboard files are empty. Its place here is as a small example of exploring a subject I enjoy through data.
